About us

Our story

Glow Hair Salon is a locally owned hair and beauty business serving clients since 2018. We started with a small team and grew through quality service, experienced stylists and recommendations from happy clients.

Mission

To provide professional, affordable and personalised hair-care services in a welcoming environment while helping every client feel confident and valued.

Vision

To become a trusted local salon known for quality hairstyling, excellent service and a convenient digital booking experience.
